Every review here is a static, AI-assisted reading of a pull request's diff. Nothing was run. "How it was checked" on each row says what was actually done, and by whom. Rows tagged "Automated scan" come from a monitor that matches patterns in added lines: its hits are leads to look at, not conclusions.
